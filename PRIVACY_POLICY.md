# Tilt Push privacy policy

Last updated 14 November 2026.

## What the game stores

Which levels you have finished and in how many pushes, whether a level was finished with Hint, the daily levels you have finished, a level you have started (with its undo history), whether you have done the lesson, your settings (sound, vibration (kept in the record, though the app has no vibration switch at present), the landing outline, and whether to tell you when a level can no longer be finished), a note that adverts are removed if you bought that, and a note of when the last full-screen advert was shown, so the limit on adverts holds after you close the game.

## Where it is stored

Only on your device, in the game's own private storage. If you have Android's backup switched on, Android may include it in the backup kept in your own Google account, so your progress can follow you to a new phone. The makers of the game cannot see that backup.

## What the game sends

The game itself sends nothing about your play. It has no accounts and no analytics of its own.

## Adverts

After some finished levels the game shows a full-screen advert supplied by Google AdMob, and it offers an advert you can choose to watch to get a second or later Hint on a level. To choose and measure adverts, Google collects your device's advertising ID, your approximate location from your internet address, and information about your device and how you interact with the advert. This is covered by Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

A full-screen advert appears only after a level you have just finished. There is none in the lesson, none until you have finished more than ten levels, at most one every three minutes, and never because you were stuck, restarted or took a move back. The advert for a Hint is always your own choice.

## Your advert choices

In Europe and the UK you are asked for your consent before adverts are shown, and you can change your answer at any time under Settings, Advert privacy choices (that row is shown only in those places). On any Android phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the phone's Settings under Privacy, Ads. With no internet connection the game works as normal and shows no adverts.

## Removing adverts

You can pay once for "Remove ads": full-screen adverts are removed for good and every Hint is free. The payment is handled entirely by Google Play: the game never sees your card or payment details. Google Play remembers the purchase for your Google account, so Restore purchase in Settings brings it back on a new phone. The game keeps a note on your device that adverts are removed.

## Deleting your data

Clear the game's storage in your phone's app settings, or uninstall the game.

## Changes

If a later version changes what is collected, this policy will be updated first.
